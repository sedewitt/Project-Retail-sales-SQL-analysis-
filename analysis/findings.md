Retail Sales Analysis Findings
Sales Overview

The retail sales dataset contains 239 orders from 25 unique customers.

Across all orders:

360 total units were sold.
$41,204.40 in total revenue was generated.
The average product price was approximately $235.59.
The highest-priced product was the Laptop ($899.99).
The lowest-priced product was the Notebook Pack ($12.99).

Key Business Insights

The SQL analysis revealed several notable patterns:

Laptop sales generated the most revenue, despite the laptop having the lowest unit sales among the five products.
Staplers had the highest unit volume, showing that high sales volume does not necessarily result in the highest revenue.
Electronics generated the most category revenue, contributing $24,479.28.
Regional sales were evenly distributed across East, North, South, and West.
Cash was the most frequently used payment method, while Debit Card generated the most payment-method revenue.
Customer spending was concentrated among several high-spending customers, with four customers tied for the highest total spending.
Conclusion

This project used SQL to analyze a retail sales dataset and answer business questions related to sales performance, products, customers, regions, and payment methods.

The analysis demonstrates how SQL can transform raw transaction data into organized information that can be used to identify sales patterns and support business decision-making.
